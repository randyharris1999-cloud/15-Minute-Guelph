    How the score works

    Walking times follow Guelph's streets and trails at 4.8 km/h. Each item earns full points when it is close and fades to zero further away.

    Shops, clinics, cafés and playgrounds come from OpenStreetMap, which volunteers keep up to date, so a few places may be missing or out of date.

    Data: City of Guelph Open Data (addresses, buildings, trees, parks, watercourses, schools, facilities, restrooms, gardens, streets, trails) · Guelph Transit GTFS, fall 2026 schedule · Shops and services © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
    An independent project built on City of Guelph open data. Not an official City of Guelph service.
